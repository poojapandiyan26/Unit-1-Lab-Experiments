## UI-UX-Experiment 1

## Title: Hands-on UI/UX Experiments

## Aim:
To study the fundamentals of User Interface (UI) and User Experience (UX), differentiate UI and UX using real-world case studies, understand their relationship in design, and explore various UI/UX design tools.

## Algorithm:
1. Study the basic concepts and fundamentals of User Interface (UI) and User Experience (UX).
2. Identify the major principles of UI design, such as layout, colour, typography, consistency and accessibility.
3. Understand UX principles, including user research, usability, navigation, wireframing and prototyping.
4. Compare UI and UX based on their features, objectives and functions using real-world examples such as Google Search, Amazon and food delivery applications.
5. Illustrate the relationship between UI and UX and understand how they work together to create an effective digital product.
6. Explore different UI/UX design tools such as Figma, Adobe XD, Sketch, Balsamiq, Miro, Canva and InVision.
7. Analyse the features and applications of each design tool.
8. Summarise the importance of UI and UX in developing user-friendly digital products.

## Output:
The fundamentals of UI and UX were studied successfully. The differences between UI and UX were identified using real-world case studies. Their relationship in digital product design was illustrated, and various UI/UX design tools were explored with their features and applications.

Thus, the fundamentals of UI and UX, their differences, relationship in design and various UI/UX design tools were studied successfully.# Unit-1-Lab-Experiments
